Scope: the assigned v2 report and this folder only. Initial repository cut `eda5bb9f871a85613cfb4eda7d40dc741e55d0b5`; integrated main cut `3ac3ab7d`; literature checked 27 September 2026. No individual genetic data, reserved survey wave, CALC or project model parameter was opened or changed.

## Material adjudications

- MexVar's 42,769 are curated genotyped variants with heterogeneous evidence. The local-frequency result is distinct from prior drug-response evidence and from prospective benefit of a new panel.
- The MUC19 0.305 figure concerns MXL introgressed tracts, not a verified population carrier fraction. Supplementary carrier denominators and health function remain unresolved. A public PMC page returned a browser reCAPTCHA; the previously archived original-paper reading in `forense/analisis/dominios/lectura-genomica-v1_0.tsv` and DOI were used, with no new carrier calculation.
- MCPS CH odds compare selected exome cohorts. The September 2026 within-family study adds stronger evidence for some physical/metabolic outcomes and no within-family education signal; the paper expressly allows social mediation of some ancestry effects. No behavioral ancestry model is proposed.
- FDA K260235 is a 12 May 2026 U.S. substantial-equivalence decision for Datar's exact tissue assay. It does not establish the v1's survival claim, a 2025 clearance, Mexican authorization or reimbursement.
- The current consolidated private-sector data law, last reformed 14 November 2025, explicitly defines genetic information as sensitive. The original 20 March text is publication history, not assumed current law. The legal discussion is scoped to what the official text supports.
- The historical IMARC edition used by v1 was unavailable. Its public current edition has a different base year and horizon and does not disclose a reproducible market method.
